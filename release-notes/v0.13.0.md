## Marquee 0.13.0 · Marquee Festive 2.15.0

**A new kids' picture maker** (Names in Lights, when kids' pictures are on)
- Kids move and zoom their picture so their face fills a circle.
- The background is cut away on the phone, and they pick a look: **Cartoon Me** (a bold cartoon on a festive background), **Make Me an Elf**, **Snowman** or **Santa's Helper**, or a real photo if you allow it.
- They see their finished picture, and how it will look on your actual screen, before sending.
- Still made entirely on the kid's phone: only the finished picture is sent, never the photo. To cut away the background, the phone downloads a free tool (Google's MediaPipe) from jsDelivr the first time; if it can't, a soft oval around the face is used.

**Easier setup**
- **Look picture cards:** Appearance → Customize → Look shows a picture of each look to tap, instead of a list of names.
- **Your website's look and words:** Marquee → Settings → Setup home has buttons that open Customize right at the look, the top of the page, banner messages, Before You Visit tips, Kids' Corner and donations.
- **No garland beside section titles:** the strands of garland on each side of the section titles (Kids' Corner, Vote for Your Favorite Song…) are gone, in every look. They looked odd sliding over the page picture as you scrolled.

**More switches in Show Control and the Show Manager**
- **Kids' pictures:** turn kids' pictures off for a night (or on again) without changing your settings. Only shows once pictures are set up in Settings.
- **"Show on" banner:** off, the banner only shows when you cancel a night.
- **Make a Light Show, Coloring pages, Games:** each part of the Kids' Corner on its own.
- **Guestbook:** turn the guestbook on or off.
