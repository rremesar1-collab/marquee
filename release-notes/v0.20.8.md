## Marquee 0.20.8 · Marquee Festive 2.23.8

- **Your house is found from the address:** type your street address in Show details → Location & weather and the pin goes on the house itself, not the middle of your ZIP code or town. You don't need Google Maps or the Find button. It looks the address up when you leave the box, press Enter or save.
- **A heads-up when the pin is off:** if your show's location is only the middle of your ZIP code or town, Settings tells you, both under the address and next to "Only at the show". It also shows how to copy your exact spot from Google Maps.
- **Only at the show works right away:** turning it on or off takes effect immediately, even if your site has a page cache. Phones also get a fresh location every time a name is sent, instead of reusing an old one.
