**Marquee 0.9.0**

Your Name in Lights is much easier to set up.

- **A setup panel** at the top of Settings → Names & FPP shows each step and turns green by itself as your FPP reports in: FPP connected, your screen, the names break, which playlist, and a test name. Anything not done yet has a **Show me** button that takes you to the right spot.
- **Pick your screen from a list.** Your FPP sends its Pixel Overlay Models (with their size), so there's nothing to type and no typos.
- **Check again** reruns your FPP's setup check from the website, and it reruns by itself when you change your screens or playlists.
- **Send a test name** puts a name on your screens right away.
- **No names break sequence needed.** If your FPP doesn't have a names break playlist, Marquee makes a simple one: the show pauses, the names go up on your screens (with your names break audio, if you built it), then the show carries on. If you already have a names break playlist, it's never touched. You can turn this off under "Make a simple names break for me".
- **Names only during your show.** If your FPP schedules more than one playlist (like a show and a static display), Marquee suggests your show playlist. One click and names wait for it.
- A tip if names would scroll too fast to read on your screen, with the fix.
- **Use my own names break audio.** Made your own names break audio? Turn this on (card 3) and Marquee leaves it in your playlists and puts the names up at the times you set in card 1, instead of putting its own audio in.
- **Put it in line** adds a test name to the line from your computer, voice and all, so you can test the names break without sending names from your phone.

**Fixes for show nights** (update the Marquee plugin on your FPP to get them):
- The first name of a break goes up right on time: your FPP no longer misses the start of a break while it's talking to your website (that happens in the background now).
- A switched-off or unplugged screen no longer makes every name, and Santa's voice, run several seconds late.
- Spoken names are downloaded as soon as they come in, so the first name of a break is on time.
- Names stay in step with the announcer: a late name skips its turn instead of doubling up, and Santa no longer talks over himself or the outro.
- If one of your themes uses your regular Names in Lights playlist, names now follow your names break audio's timing.
- If a screen is set to the raw HDMI model ("FB - HDMI-A-1"), names blank your sequence while they're up. The setup check now says so and tells you which model to pick instead, so names go up on top of your sequence.

Update the **Marquee plugin on your FPP** too (Content Setup → Plugin Manager) to get the screen list, Check again, test names and the simple names break. Everything keeps working if you only update one of them.
