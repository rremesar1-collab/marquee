**Marquee 0.9.3**

- **Names break after every few songs** (Settings → Names & FPP → card 1). Busy night? Pick 2, 3 or 4 songs and names collect while your show plays, then go up together in one break, instead of a break after every song. If 6 names are waiting, the break comes sooner so nobody waits too long. Thanks to Steve for the idea!

Update the **Marquee plugin on your FPP** too, or download the new marquee_names.py if you put the script on by hand.
