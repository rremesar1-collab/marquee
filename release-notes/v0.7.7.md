**Weather radar for shows outside the US.** Shows in Australia, Canada, the UK and everywhere else now get a live radar map and a "View Full Weather Forecast" button (from Windy.com), in °C and km/h for metric countries. US shows keep the National Weather Service radar.

Also:
- "Only at the show" for Your Name in Lights is set in kilometres for metric countries (1 km suits most shows).
