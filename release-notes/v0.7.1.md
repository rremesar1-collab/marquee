**New: "Only at the show" for Your Name in Lights.** Turn it on under Marquee → Settings → Features and choose a distance (0.5 mile suits most shows). Kids' phones share their location when they send a name; it is only compared with your show's address and never saved. You and your Show helpers can still send test names from anywhere. There's also a switch for it in the Show Manager's Names tab.

Also:
- A small "Show owner" link at the bottom of every page opens the Show Manager.
- The Show Manager page opens even if your site's saved page addresses are out of date.

Festive theme users: update to `marquee-festive-2.9.7.zip` too.
