## Marquee 0.12.9 · Marquee Festive 2.14.5

- **Coloring pages: a way back.** The print page has a "← Back to Coloring Pages" button next to Print, and it opens in the same tab, so the phone's back button works too. The print window no longer pops up by itself; it opens when you tap Print. The words on the page are in the visitor's language (English, Spanish or French).
