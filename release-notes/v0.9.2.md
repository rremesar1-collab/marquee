**Marquee 0.9.2**

- **Fix: the same name going up after every song.** If your website address in the FPP's settings was slightly different from your site's real address (http instead of https, or with or without www), your website quietly redirected the FPP and the "name shown" message was lost on the way. Names kept coming back, and the setup panel never updated. The names script (3.11.1) now follows the redirect without losing anything.

Update the **Marquee plugin on your FPP**, or download the new marquee_names.py (and marquee_names.json) if you put the script on by hand.
