## Marquee 0.20.5 · Marquee Festive 2.23.5

- **Canadian and UK spelling:** in WordPress, go to Settings → General → Site Language and pick **English (Canada)** or **English (UK)**. The whole website, Settings and the Show Manager switch to colour, favourite, neighbours, cancelled and centre, and "ZIP code" becomes "Postal code" (Canada) or "Postcode" (UK). The Kids' Corner "Colouring Pages" door changes too.
- UK English also uses customise and synchronise. Australia, New Zealand, Ireland and South Africa get the UK spelling.
- US English sites don't change. The Español and Français buttons work the same as before, and picking English again keeps your spelling.
