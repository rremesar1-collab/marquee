## Marquee 0.15.0 · Marquee Festive 2.18.0 · names script 3.15.2

- **Listen on Your Phone:** a new gold button in Tonight's Show, on its own line right under the radio station and show hours, for visitors who'd rather hear the music on their phone. Paste your listening link in Marquee → Settings → Show details, or Appearance → Customize → Show Hours & Status. For PulseMesh's free plan use https://player.pulsemesh.io (it finds shows near the visitor); with a PulseMesh Display Code, use its link so phones go straight to your show. Leave it empty and your site stays exactly as it was.
- **QR code (optional):** tick "Also show a QR code for it on computers" in Customize and a QR code appears next to the button for people looking at your site on a computer. Phones just see the button.
- Works with the Festive theme and with any other theme (Marquee blocks), and is translated into Spanish and French.
- **Names script 3.15.2 (FPP):** with 1 or 2 names waiting, the short names break could let the last name run into the announcer's closing ("if you want your name in lights, visit..."), mostly when Names per break is set under 6. The script was counting more name spots than the short break's audio has. Now the short breaks use exactly their own spots: 2 names are each said twice (or once each with fewer names per break), 1 name twice, always before the closing. The full break is unchanged.
- README: spoken names (ElevenLabs) work on FPP 9 too; they only need FPP's Play Media command. Only kids' pictures need FPP 10.
