## Marquee 0.18.0 · Marquee Festive 2.20.0

- **Kids' Corner doors blend into your page.** The four door pictures now have see-through edges, so your background (and its lights) shows all around them instead of a dark box. The gold border stays, each door stands in a soft pile of snow, and the shadow and the glow when you point at a door follow the door's shape. Thanks to the show owner who suggested it!
- **Door signs fixed.** "At showtime" and "Just for fun" now fully cover the sign painted on the door, so "On now!" and "Win a prize!" no longer peek out underneath.
- **New: Suggest an idea.** Marquee → Suggest an idea (also in Settings → Help). Pick the kind of idea, write it, add a screenshot if you like and your email for an answer, and it's sent to Marquee Support straight from your website. No email app needed. The ideas you've sent are listed under the form.
- **Your own door pictures:** a PNG with see-through edges blends in best.
- Includes the names script for FPP 3.16.3 (the first name waits the full "every N songs").
- In English, Spanish and French.
