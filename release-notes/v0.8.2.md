**Marquee 0.8.2 · Marquee Festive 2.11.4**

### Get help from Marquee Support
- New **Marquee → Get help**: tell Marquee Support what's wrong and a support report goes with it (your setup and your FPP's setup check, never your keys or passwords).
- You choose whether Marquee Support can sign in to fix it with you, for 24 hours or until you end it. They only get Marquee's pages and your media library: no plugins, themes, users or WordPress settings.
- Chat right on the page, with pictures (paste a screenshot). You get an email when Marquee Support answers.
- Every setting Marquee Support changes shows up in the chat in plain words, with a summary when the session ends. End it any time, or give 24 more hours.
- With the new names script, Marquee Support can also see your FPP's status, playlists, screens and log, and send a test name to your screens.

### Names on FPP 10.1
- **Fixed:** on FPP 10.1, names were accepted but never went up, even with your show playlist playing from the scheduler. FPP 10.1 calls a playing playlist "playing media", and the script took that to mean no playlist was running. Names script 3.6.1 checks the playlist itself.

### Show me how
- Connect your FPP, Connect Remote Falcon and Say each name out loud are now guided walkthroughs on the real Settings page, with every step in order: turning names on, the names break playlist, saving before you download, FPP 10's interface level, starting your show as a playlist, and a test name. They follow the names mode you picked.
- The setup guide and walkthrough now say to set FPP's User Interface Level to Advanced first (FPP 10 hides "Run Script" otherwise), and that Command Presets is under Status/Control.

### Update your FPP script
Download **marquee_names.py 3.6.1** from Settings → Names & FPP → Download both files, and replace the old one on your FPP. If you're on FPP 10.1, this is the fix for names that never show up.
