## Marquee 0.12.0 · Marquee Festive 2.13.0

- **Your own words in every language:** with the language button on (Marquee → Settings → Website basics → Languages, two or more ticked), Appearance → Customize has an extra box under each of your texts for each other language: banner messages, Before You Visit tips, section titles, button words and more. Visitors who pick that language see what you typed there. Leave a box empty and your main wording shows.
