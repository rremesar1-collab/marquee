## Marquee 0.14.4 · Marquee Festive 2.16.4

- **Hide just the home page card:** new switch in Customize → Songs Page: Favorite Song Voting, "Show the Show Videos & Voting card on the home page". Off hides only the card; the Songs & Videos page, the videos and the voting keep working from the Vote button in the menu.
- **The card says what's on:** with only videos it reads "Show Videos" and Watch; with only voting it reads "Vote for Your Favorite Song" and Vote; with both, "Show Videos & Voting".
- **Look and your own photo:** the looks now only change the picture behind the page, and your own Background photo replaces it in every look. Customize → Look now says so when you have your own photo, and how to remove it to see the looks.
