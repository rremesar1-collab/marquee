**Country on the Location step.** Marquee → Settings → Location now shows your show's country (Find fills it in from your address) and a line saying where your weather and radar come from: the National Weather Service in the US, or a live Windy.com radar map everywhere else.

Also:
- Shows outside the US always get the Windy.com radar, even if a US radar station was left in the settings.
