## Marquee 0.13.2 · Marquee Festive 2.15.2

- **Kids' picture maker: center any face.** With a tall phone photo, the picture could only move up and down. Now it moves every way and can zoom out a little, so a face near the edge can be put in the middle of the circle. Past the edge of the photo you see a soft, blurred copy of it, and it's never part of the cartoon.
