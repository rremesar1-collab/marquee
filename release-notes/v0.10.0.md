**Marquee 0.10.0 · Marquee Festive 2.12.0**

- **Kids' pictures on your screens.** Off unless you turn it on (Marquee → Settings → Names & FPP → Kids' pictures). A kid adds a picture to their name: a cartoon of themselves (made on their own phone, so only the cartoon is sent) or a real photo, whichever you allow. A parent or guardian has to tick a box first, and every picture waits for you or a Show helper to approve it in Show Control or the Show Manager. In the names break the kid gets two turns: their picture while the voice says their name, then their name. Pictures are deleted right after they're shown, and always within 2 hours. Please read the notice in Settings before turning it on. Needs FPP 10 or newer and names script 3.14.
- **Automatic check for kids' pictures (optional).** Add your own free Google Cloud Vision key and turn on "Check pictures automatically": a picture that's clean, shows exactly one face and has no words in it goes up without waiting for you. Anything else still waits in Show Control, with the reason. About 330 pictures a month are free with Google.
- **A switch for each Kids' Corner part.** In Settings → Features, Your Name in Lights, Make a Light Show (and its contest), Coloring Pages and Games now sit under Kids' Corner, each with its own switch. Turn on just the ones you want; the Kids' Corner page and the card on the home page show only those.
- **Spanish and French.** Your website follows the language you pick in Settings → General → Site Language. Pick Español or Français and the whole site switches: the home page, Kids' Corner, song voting, alerts and the Show Manager, plus Marquee's own settings screens. Visitors don't pick a language; they see the one you set. English sites look exactly the same as before.
- **Two new looks for Marquee Festive.** Christmas Village (sapphire and gold, a lit village by the river) and Santa's North Pole (red and gold, Santa's workshop and sleigh). Pick one in Appearance → Customize → Show Website → Look. Your show name sits on the big picture as real text, the Kids' Corner and donation boxes get their own pictures, and there's a "Your Name in Lights" sign on the home page. On phones the picture shows in full with your show name under it. Classic stays the default.
- **YouTube links for show videos.** In My Sequences, paste a YouTube link in a video's Video box instead of uploading the MP4. The card on the Songs & Videos page shows the YouTube picture, and the video plays in YouTube's player in the pop-up. Handy when your website limits how big an upload can be.
- **Song covers.** Tonight's Playlist and Vote for Your Favorite Song now show a small cover next to each song. Marquee uses the artwork you set for the song in Remote Falcon. For songs without artwork, it finds the album cover by song and artist and saves a copy on your site (only when the song matches, so you won't get the wrong one).
- **Your own covers.** Settings → Remote Falcon → Song covers lists every song with its cover. Pick your own picture, choose No cover, or go back to Automatic. You can turn covers off there too.

Texts you already typed in (your banner messages, tips, button words) stay as you wrote them. To switch them to the new language, change them in Customize.
