## Marquee 0.18.4 · Marquee Festive 2.21.1

- **Long playlists scroll inside their own box.** With more than 12 songs, Tonight's Playlist stays a fixed height and visitors scroll the songs inside it, so the rest of the page is right there. A line above the list says how many songs there are tonight, and the box moves by itself to the song playing now. 12 songs or fewer look the same as before. Thanks to Patrick for the idea!
- In English, Spanish and French.
