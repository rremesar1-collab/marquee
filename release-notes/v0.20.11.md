## Marquee 0.20.11 · Marquee Festive 2.23.11

- **Pick the Next Song hides when picking is off:** when Viewer Control is turned off in Remote Falcon, the button disappears from your home page, and comes back by itself within about 30 seconds of turning it on. You still see it while you're logged in, with a note saying visitors can't.
- **Rather show the "closed" message?** In Settings → Connections → Remote Falcon, set "When song picking is off" to "Show it with my message and the song list".
