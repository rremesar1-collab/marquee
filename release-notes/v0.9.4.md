**Marquee 0.9.4**

- **Fix: a name said out loud but not shown on the screen.** With Scroll speed set to Slow, a long message could take longer than its turn. When it finished, FPP cleared the screen and wiped out the next name with it. Each scrolling name now always finishes within its own turn (Slow uses the whole turn).

Update the **Marquee plugin on your FPP**, or download the new marquee_names.py if you put the script on by hand.
