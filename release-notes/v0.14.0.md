## Marquee 0.14.0 · Marquee Festive 2.16.0

**Busy nights**
- **Names each phone can send:** new setting in Marquee → Settings → Names in Lights → Names break, from 1 to 5 names an hour (3, as before). So one family can't fill a whole names break. You skip the limit while logged in, for testing.
- **Names break after every, from your phone:** the setting (1 to 6 songs) is now in Show Control and in the Show Manager's Names tab. When lots of names come in, switch it from 1 song to 3 songs: names collect and go up together, instead of a names break after every song.

**Looks**
- **Snow on the Win a Prize box:** in the Kids' Corner's Make a Light Show, the contest prize box has snow across the top like every other box, in all three looks.
