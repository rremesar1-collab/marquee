## Marquee 0.20.1 · Marquee Festive 2.23.1

Fixes and small additions from show owners' testing (thanks, Patrick!).

- **Show Facts in every language:** the page title, the headline and line on the photo, the "Did You Know?" and "Fan Favorites" titles, and each fact card have "Other languages" boxes. The two part titles can be changed now too.
- **Countdown words while the show is on:** Home page → Countdown → "Words while the show is on" replaces "The Show Is On Right Now — Tune to…" (with its own words in each language).
- **Song voting:** the Pick Your Favorite list is taller, about 8 songs before it scrolls, on phones too.
- **Tonight's Playlist:** a "Vote for your favorite" button that goes straight to song voting.
- **Box colors:** when every box is one color (Look & colors → Box colors), the glow around each box follows that color too. No more green glow on a red site.
- **Get the App:** the whole box can be switched off (Home page → Get the App).
- **Names in Lights on big LED matrices:** matrices up to 256 pixels tall now get big matrix-style letters (before, anything over 64 pixels tall got small TV-style text). Each screen also has **Bigger or smaller** to fine-tune the size. Needs the Marquee plugin for FPP 3.17 (update it in FPP's Plugin Manager).
- In English, Spanish and French.
