## Marquee 0.14.5 · Marquee Festive 2.17.0 · names script 3.15.1

- **Box colors:** new setting in Appearance → Customize → Look → Box colors: Classic mix (red, green and blue), All red, All green or All blue. It works with any look and only changes the box colors; the snow, bulbs, spacing and layout stay the same. The status banner keeps its own colors (on, canceled, off season).
- **Names script 3.15.1 (FPP):** just after switching from names during songs to a names break between songs, the short breaks for 1 or 2 names weren't made yet, so 2 names went into the full break and repeated to fill it. Now the short breaks are made first. Also, when the same line repeats in the log (like "could not read FPP status" while the FPP is busy), it's written once with a count instead of flooding the log.
