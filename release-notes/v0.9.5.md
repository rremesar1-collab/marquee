**Marquee 0.9.5 · Marquee Festive 2.11.9**

- **Check for updates now.** There's a new link under Marquee on the Plugins page. Click it and Marquee asks GitHub right away, so a new version shows up at once instead of within 6 hours.
- **"Names break after every" now shows everywhere.** If your names break comes after every 2, 3 or 4 songs, the names form tells kids about how many songs until their name goes up instead of "after this song", Show Control shows when the next break is, and the phone buzz comes when their names break is actually next. Thanks to Steve for catching it!

Update the **Marquee plugin on your FPP** too, or download the new marquee_names.py if you put the script on by hand.
