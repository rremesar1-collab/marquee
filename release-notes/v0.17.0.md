## Marquee 0.17.0 · Marquee Festive 2.19.0

- **New: Thank You to Our Sponsors.** A box on your home page for the local businesses that support your show. It sits full width between the guestbook and Donate / Stay Connected, with the same lights, snow and title ribbon as the rest of the page, in all three looks.
- **Easy to fill in.** Turn it on in Marquee → Settings → Features & pages, then add sponsors in Sharing & donations: pick each logo from your Media Library, type the business name, and add their website if you like (clicking the logo opens it). An optional short line under the name thanks them for something specific. Use the arrows to change the order.
- **Your words.** Change the title, keep or clear the thank-you line under it, and add an email address or web page for "Want to sponsor the show? Get in touch". Leave that empty and the line is hidden.
- **Your color, your switch.** Pick green, red or blue for the box, and turn it on or off any time from Show Control or the Show Manager on your phone, like the guestbook. If Box colors is set to one color for every box, that color wins.
- **Any logo looks right.** Every logo sits on a light card, so dark logos and JPGs with a white background stay readable. Rows even out by themselves (6 sponsors make 3 + 3, 7 make 4 + 3), and phones show 2 per row.
- **Nothing changes until you use it.** It's off to start, and with no sponsors the box isn't there at all.
- Also a Sponsors block and `[marquee section="sponsors"]` for shows using another theme. In English, Spanish and French.
