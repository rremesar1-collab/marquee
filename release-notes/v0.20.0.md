## Marquee 0.20.0 · Marquee Festive 2.23.0

- **New: make your own pages.** Pages → Add New now offers layouts in your show's colors to start from: **Our Story**, **Questions & Answers**, **Photo Gallery**, **Meet the Team** and **Give Back**. Change the words and photos, move the boxes around, or add more.
- **Show Boxes:** a box in your show's colors (red, green, blue or night) with snow on top and lights around it. Put anything inside: words, photos, videos, columns, buttons. Ready-made kinds: Photo & Words, People (round photos with names), Then & Now, Questions (visitors tap to read the answer) and Photo Gallery.
  - Each box can have a ribbon title above it, and you can switch its snow and lights off.
  - **See-through for each box:** follow your website's setting, or give one box its own amount with a slider (Solid to 50%).
  - The boxes change with your look: Classic, Christmas Village or Santa's North Pole.
- **Live Camera block, with its own settings:** use your live camera or a different YouTube link (a second camera, or last year's show), its own title and words, show it only during show hours, and pick the box color and see-through.
- **A button at the top for your pages:** a new page made with Show Boxes gets its own button next to Kids' Corner and Show Facts. Turn it off in the page's "Top of your website" setting. On phones only four buttons fit, so the rest go under **More**.
- All the live parts of your show (countdown, playlist, weather, guestbook…) can go on your pages too.
- In English, Spanish and French.
