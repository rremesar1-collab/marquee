**Marquee 0.11.0 · Marquee Festive 2.12.1**

- **A language button for your visitors.** In a bilingual area? Marquee → Settings → Website basics → Languages: tick two or more of English, Español and Français, and a small button shows in the top corner of your website (at the bottom on phones). Each visitor picks their language and it's remembered on their phone. The whole site switches: the status sign, Tonight's Show, the menu, Kids' Corner and the names form, the live updates and the dates. Your website still starts in your WordPress Site Language, and WP Admin stays in your own language.
- **Words you typed yourself stay as you wrote them** (your banner messages, tips and button words in Customize). Texts that are still the standard wording, even if you saved them in Customize, now switch with the language.
- **Using a page cache plugin?** Add the cookie `mq_lang` to its list of cookies that skip the cache, so each visitor sees their own language.
