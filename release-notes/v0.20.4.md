## Marquee 0.20.4 · Marquee Festive 2.23.4

- **New pictures for show gear:** 17 new pictures for your number boxes, Before You Visit tips and Did You Know? cards: LED panel, TV, projector, controller, computer, pixels (a string of lights), power, plug, speaker, FM transmitter and Wi-Fi, plus calendar, people, snowflake, candy cane and arch. In Settings, the chosen picture now shows next to each list, so you can see it before you save.
- **Headline above the photo:** in Show Facts → "Where the headline goes", the top choice is now "Above the photo". The headline and line sit in a dark strip just above your photo, so they never cover your display, even when there's little sky above the roof.
- **Did You Know? cards match the home page:** in the Boxes style, each card now has the picture on top, then the gold words, then the fact, centered, like the Before You Visit tips on the home page.
