## Marquee 0.19.0 · Marquee Festive 2.22.0

- **New: Show Facts.** A page about your show, with its own button at the top next to Kids' Corner. Turn it on and fill it in under Marquee → Settings → Show Facts. Thanks to Patrick for the idea!
  - **Your show:** a photo of your display that you upload, a headline in script across it, and your Behind the Lights numbers counting up underneath.
  - **Did You Know?** fact cards with a picture, a few big words ("40 hours") and the fact.
  - **Fan Favorites:** tap a year to see that year's top 5 songs, each with a string of lights as long as its votes. Years with song voting fill in by themselves, and they're kept when you clear the votes for a new season. Type in the top songs for years before you had voting.
  - **Two styles:** Photo (a big photo of your show with the numbers under it) or Boxes (number boxes, small fact cards together, and each year's favorites side by side). Pick one with a picture of each in the settings.
  - Show any or all three parts; a hidden part's title goes too. Switch the page on or off from Show Control or the Show Manager on your phone.
- **Settings, one page at a time.** Marquee → Settings is reorganized so everything for a page is in one place:
  - **Your show:** Start here, Show details (name, radio, logo, place, dates and hours) and Connections (Remote Falcon, Facebook and Instagram).
  - **Your pages:** Home page, Songs & Videos, Kids' Corner, Names in Lights and Show Facts. The Home page lists every box from top to bottom (banner, countdown, playlist, tips, Behind the Lights, guestbook, sponsors, donate and the rest). Each box has its on/off switch right on it, and opens to show all its words and settings.
  - Settings that were in two places now live in one. Your words in other languages are under each text box ("Other languages").
  - **Appearance → Customize keeps just the look:** the look, box colors, see-through, falling snow and your background photo (the Look & colors button in Settings opens it). Show Control and the Show Manager on your phone don't change. The Show me how tours follow the new layout.
  - Nothing to redo: all your settings stay as they are.
- **Long playlists scroll inside their own box.** With more than 12 songs, Tonight's Playlist stays a fixed height and visitors scroll the songs inside it. The box moves by itself to the song playing now.
- Also a Show Facts block and `[marquee section="facts"]` for other themes. In English, Spanish and French.
