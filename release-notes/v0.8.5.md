**Marquee 0.8.5 · Marquee Festive 2.11.6**

### Pick the Next Song (Remote Falcon)
Visitors can now vote for or request the next song right on your website. Add your Remote Falcon viewer page in **Settings → Remote Falcon** (copy it from Remote Falcon → Control Panel → View Viewer Page) and Tonight's Show gets a **Pick the Next Song** button while the show is running.

- It shows your Remote Falcon songs, what's playing now, and the vote counts (voting mode) or the songs in line (jukebox mode).
- Your Remote Falcon rules still apply to every visitor: one pick each, queue size, and the location check.
- You see the button any time while logged in, so you can check it before opening night.

### Countdown to Christmas, Halloween or your own date
**Settings → Schedule → Countdown**: Christmas, Halloween or a date you pick (like New Year's Eve). Automatic picks Halloween when your last show night is in August to October. The line under the countdown says "12 days until Halloween", "Halloween is tomorrow!" and "Happy Halloween!".

### Also
- Your site's scripts now refresh in visitors' browsers whenever they change, so updates show up right away.
