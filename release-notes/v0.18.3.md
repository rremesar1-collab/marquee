## Marquee 0.18.3 · Marquee Festive 2.21.0

- **New: see-through boxes and titles.** In Appearance → Customize → Look, slide "See-through boxes and titles" from Solid (how it looks now) up to 50% and watch the preview change as you slide. Your page picture shows through the boxes and the section titles, while the words, light-string borders, snow and glow stay bright and easy to read. Works with every look (Classic, Christmas Village, Santa's North Pole) and every box color. Thanks to Patrick for the idea!
- In English, Spanish and French.
