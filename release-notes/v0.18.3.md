## Marquee 0.18.3 · Marquee Festive 2.21.0

- **New: see-through boxes and titles.** In Appearance → Customize → Look, pick Solid (how it looks now), A little see-through, or More see-through. Your page picture shows through the boxes and the section titles, while the words, light-string borders, snow and glow stay bright and easy to read. Works with every look (Classic, Christmas Village, Santa's North Pole) and every box color. Thanks to Patrick for the idea!
- In English, Spanish and French.
