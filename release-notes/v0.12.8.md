## Marquee 0.12.8 · Marquee Festive 2.14.4

- **Song voting on phones:** once a song with a long name (like "Tony Bennett - Santa Claus Is Coming To Town") got votes, the Leaderboard made both voting boxes wider than the screen, so the vote buttons and the right side were cut off. Long names now get "…" in the Leaderboard and wrap in the song list, and no box can grow past the edge of the page.
