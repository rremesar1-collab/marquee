## Marquee 0.18.2 · Marquee Festive 2.20.1

- **Guestbook spam goes to Spam on its own.** Robot messages no longer pile up in Pending: anything sent without the form, sent in a few seconds, sent without anyone typing, or with links or spam words goes straight to Comments → Spam. Real visitors don't see anything different. Thanks for the request!
- **Cleanup:** robot messages already waiting in Pending are moved to Spam the first time you open your dashboard after updating, and guestbook spam is emptied after 30 days.
- **Optional "I'm not a robot" check:** if some still get through, add a free Cloudflare Turnstile check in Customize → Guestbook (works with the WordPress guestbook).
- In English, Spanish and French.
