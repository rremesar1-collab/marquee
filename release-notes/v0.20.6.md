## Marquee 0.20.6 · Marquee Festive 2.23.6

- **Weather in your language:** the weather words (Partly Cloudy, Rain, Snow…) now show in Spanish and French too.
- **Weather on phones:** long weather words no longer push the weather box wider than the screen.
- **Smallest phones:** on narrow phones, or with bigger text turned on, the show hours in Tonight's Show wrap neatly instead of making the page slide sideways.
