## Marquee 0.20.6 · Marquee Festive 2.23.6

- **Weather in your language:** the weather words (Partly Cloudy, Rain, Snow…) now show in Spanish and French too.
- **Weather on phones:** long weather words no longer push the weather box wider than the screen.
- **Weather radar:** it no longer stretches into a tall strip when Tonight's Show next to it is taller (for example with the Pick the Next Song box). The weather sits in the middle of its box instead.
- **Show hours:** they always stay inside their box. On narrow screens the day wraps onto its own line and the times stay together.
