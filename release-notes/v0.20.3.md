## Marquee 0.20.3 · Marquee Festive 2.23.3

- **Show Facts, Boxes style:** the photo of your show now shows in the Boxes style too, with your headline and line on it, above the number boxes. (Before, only the Photo style showed it.)
- **Show Facts photo keeps its own shape:** a wide night photo of your house now shows whole (mega tree and all) instead of being zoomed in and cut off at the sides, and the photo box is no taller than it needs to be. On phones, the headline and line go just under the photo so they don't cover it.
- **Where the headline goes:** Show Facts → "Where the headline goes": on the photo at the top (a night photo's dark sky), at the bottom, or under the photo, so the words never cover your display.
