**Marquee 0.11.1 · Marquee Festive 2.12.2**

- **Pop-ups readable with any theme.** On sites using a theme other than Marquee Festive, the Pick the Next Song box (and the show video and Kids' Show Wall pop-ups) could come up as black text on a dark background, when the theme or a page builder moves them out of the page. They now carry their own look with them, wherever they end up.
