**New: Instagram and YouTube links.** Add them next to Facebook and TikTok in Marquee → Settings → Your show. Each link has its own Show switch, so you can turn any button on or off without deleting the link. The buttons appear in Stay Connected and as small icons at the bottom of every page.

Festive theme users: update to Marquee Festive 2.9.9 too, for the new icons at the bottom of the page and the Instagram and YouTube button colors (WordPress offers it under Dashboard → Updates).
