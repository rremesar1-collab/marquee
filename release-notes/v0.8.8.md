**Marquee 0.8.8 · Marquee Festive 2.11.8**

- **Connect your FPP, step by step** (the slideshow on Settings → Names & FPP) and the guided tour now walk through the **Marquee plugin for FPP**: install it in FPP's Plugin Manager, paste your connection code, done. The old steps (uploading the script and settings file, Command Presets, rebooting) are gone; that way still works under "Or put the script on your FPP by hand".
- The slideshow and tour also show the new **Only during these playlists** option.
- New screenshots from FPP 10.3.
