## Marquee 0.20.14 · Marquee Festive 2.23.14

- **Reply to guestbook messages:** in the Show Manager's Approve tab, tap Reply on a message. Your reply shows under their message on your guestbook, labeled with your show's name. Replying to a waiting message approves it too. Messages from the last 3 days can get a reply or have it changed. If their phone has your show's alerts on, it gets a buzz with your reply.
- **Choose how long a phone waits between names:** a new "Then they wait" setting, next to "Names each phone can send", in Settings → Names in Lights and in the Show Manager's Names tab. Pick 1 to 24 hours. A phone that used up its names is told what time it can send again.
- **Name buzz in three steps:** when a name is a few songs away, the kid gets "Got it! About 3 songs" right away, "Get ready! After this song" when the last song before the names break starts, and "Look at the screens!" when the break starts. A name that doesn't fit in a full break is told it's in the next one.
- **FPP plugin 3.17.7:** tells your website how many names fit in a break, so only the kids going up get buzzed. Emptying the talking faces box now turns the faces off right away. Santa no longer talks over himself: a long name, or Santa answering a "Hi Santa!", gets a longer turn, and the next name waits until he has finished.
