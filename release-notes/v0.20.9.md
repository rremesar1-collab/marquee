## Marquee 0.20.9 · Marquee Festive 2.23.9

- **Close song picking for a night, with your own message:** turn off Viewer Control in Remote Falcon, and Pick the Next Song shows your message (like "Voting is closed tonight for our special opening show") with your song list, without the pick buttons. Set the message in Settings → Connections, in the Remote Falcon section. Leave it empty for the standard message. Song hearts on the Songs page keep working.
