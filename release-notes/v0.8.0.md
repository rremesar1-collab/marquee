**Marquee 0.8.0 · Marquee Festive 2.11.3**

The biggest update yet: names said out loud, themed shows kids can pick, sequences made for you, social posts, traffic alerts and a new Show Control.

### Your names, your way
- **Names & FPP** (was "Your Name in Lights") opens with a "How it works" picture and lets you choose how names go up: **between songs** in a names break, or **during songs** on a spare TV or projector (no extra sequence needed).
- **Spoken names (optional):** an ElevenLabs voice reads each name as it goes up ("Merry Christmas, Emma!"). Clips are saved, so a repeated name costs nothing. Optional "talking faces" moves your singing faces' mouths while a name is spoken, plus speaking speed and "My voice is Santa" options.
- **Your own messages:** write the messages kids pick from under "Pick your message" (up to 6, like "Feliz Navidad, NAME!"), with a preview of how each one looks on your screens. Or keep the standard ones.
- **Blocked names and words:** see the names you blocked, unblock one with a click, or add your own words. The bad-word filter is always on, with nothing to set up.
- **Names break audio:** your voice says an intro and an outro, with soft music under the names, leveled to the same loudness as your songs. Also makes short versions for 1 or 2 names. The FPP script downloads it and sets up the playlists by itself.

### Sequences made for you
- **Sequence maker:** Marquee makes your names break sequence and themed versions (Candy Cane Swirl, Snowfall, Rainbow Party… or describe your own and the AI makes it) from your xLights layout, right in your browser. Your layout file is never uploaded. Make up to 6 at once.
- **No timing to set up:** Marquee works out when each name goes up and writes it into every sequence it makes. "Use it" fills the numbers in for you.
- **Two ways to get it on your FPP:** upload the `.fseq` in FPP's File Manager, or send it straight from xLights with FPP Connect.

### Themes kids can pick
- Kids pick the show their name goes up in. Each card shows an animated house in that theme's colors, or your own picture (the Classic card can have one too).
- The FPP plays the theme the longest-waiting kid picked; names with the same theme go up together.

### Show me how
- Guided walkthroughs in Settings for the Sequence maker, Names break audio and Themes kids can pick. A pointer moves to each control with a short explanation, and the steps in xLights and on the FPP are shown as pictures.

### Social posts
- Cancel tonight's show and Marquee posts it on your **Facebook Page, Instagram and Threads**, once a night, with your picture. Optional "We're back on" post if you undo it.
- Editable messages with fill-ins, a preview, Post it now, and a posting history. Uses your own free Meta app; Settings → Social posts walks you through it.

### Traffic & parking alerts
- One tap in Show Control or the Show Manager: Heavy traffic, Driveway blocked, Please keep moving, Parking full, or your own words.
- Puts a strip at the top of every page of your website, optionally sends a phone alert and shows it on your screens. Turns itself off when the time is up.

### New Show Control
- New look: a header with tonight's status and quick actions, tiles for FPP, names, visitors, weather, social posts and phones getting alerts.
- New Traffic & parking and Social posts cards, plus more stats (names said out loud, themed picks, social posts, traffic alerts, app installs).

### Need help?
- The help assistant in Settings now lets you pick **ChatGPT or Claude**. It already sees your setup and your FPP's setup check, so you don't have to explain it. Paste either API key and Marquee works out which one it is.

### Also
- Names form on iPhones: picking a spelling suggestion now fixes the word in the name box.
- Settings show one section at a time, with step cards and status.
- Coloring pages: Remove takes a standard page out of the list; "Removed pages" lets you restore it.
- The empty "Show pages" section no longer shows when the Festive theme is active.

### Update your FPP script
Download the new **`marquee_names.py` 3.5** (in `plugin/marquee/core/fpp/`) and replace the old one on your FPP. It's needed for during-songs mode, themed shows, spoken names and traffic messages on your screens. Needs FPP 10.
